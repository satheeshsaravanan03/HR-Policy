## 3-113.1 Purpose and Summary

The University offers paid vacation to eligible staff. This policy details vacation accrual
and the procedure for requesting vacation time.

---

## 3-113.2 Scope

Eligible employees work at least 20 hours weekly (.50 FTE or higher) on fiscal-year or
academic-year schedules. Employees in non-benefits eligible, limited benefits eligible, or
temporary positions are excluded from this policy.

---

## 3-113.3 Accrual Rates

| Metric | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum hours accrued per 80-hour pay period | 6.77 hours |
| Vacation earned per hour paid | .084625 |
| Days per fiscal year (at 80 hours per pay period) | 22 |

Academic-year staff do not accrue vacation during unpaid or non-work periods.

---

## 3-113.4 Vacation Requests

Supervisors are responsible for scheduling and approving vacation before it is taken and will
accommodate requests as long as workload and department requirements permit.

---

## 3-113.5 Carry Forward Cap

Staff may carry forward up to **320 accrued hours** annually, prorated by FTE. Excess hours
are forfeited if unused by the last pay period including January 1.

---

## 3-113.6 University Holidays

Vacation time is not charged when official University holidays coincide with approved vacation.

---

## 3-113.7 Separation from Employment

Departing employees receive payment for accumulated vacation not exceeding one year's accrual,
prorated by FTE and length of service.
