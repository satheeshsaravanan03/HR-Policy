# ACME LEAVE POLICY 2026

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Eligibility

All regular full-time employees become eligible for the annual leave allowance after completing 30 calendar days of service. Contractors and temporary workers follow the terms in their engagement letter.

## 2. Annual leave allowance

Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Employees joining after 1 July receive a prorated allowance based on completed months of service.

## 3. Carry-forward

Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual-leave days into the next calendar year. Days above the 10-day limit expire on 31 March unless a written exception is approved by People Operations.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees receive 12 paid sick-leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested for an absence longer than three consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental leave

Eligible employees may take 16 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. The employee should notify People Operations at least 30 days before the expected start date where practicable.
