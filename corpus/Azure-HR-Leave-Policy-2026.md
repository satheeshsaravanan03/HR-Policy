# Azure HR Leave and Employee Access Policy 2026

**Policy ID:** AZURE-HR-2026  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01  
**Region:** Azure business unit

## 1. Scope and employee records

This policy applies to employees in the Azure business unit. Employee-specific balances and experience are read from the authorised employee record. The record is not a replacement for this policy; it supplies the current values used when calculating an answer.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

- Employees in probation or with less than one year of service receive **18 days** of annual leave per calendar year.
- Confirmed employees with one year or more of service receive **24 days** of annual leave per calendar year.

## 3. Carry-forward

- An employee with less than one year of service may carry forward up to **5 unused days** into the next calendar year.
- A confirmed employee with one year or more of service may carry forward up to **10 unused days**.
- Carry-forward available for an employee is the lower of the current unused balance and the applicable cap.

## 4. Compensatory leave

- Compensatory leave is earned only for approved work on an officially designated company holiday.
- An employee with less than one year of service may hold up to **4 compensatory-leave days**.
- A confirmed employee with one year or more of service may hold up to **6 compensatory-leave days**.
- The answer must never exceed the employee's current compensatory-leave balance or the applicable cap.

## 5. Employee-data access

Employee leave records are confidential. The assistant may return a record-based answer only for the employee identifier supplied by the requester. It must not disclose all employee records or another employee's balance, and it must identify the policy evidence used for the calculation.
