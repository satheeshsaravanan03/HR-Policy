# ACME EMPLOYMENT TERMS POLICY 2026

Effective date: 2026-03-01

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to regular full-time Acme employees. A separate written agreement may provide a longer notice period, in which case the written agreement controls.

## 2. Probationary employees

An employee who is still within the probationary period must provide 7 calendar days of written notice before resigning. Acme may waive some or all of this notice at its discretion.

## 3. Permanent employees

After successful completion of probation, a permanent employee must provide 30 calendar days of written notice before resigning. The notice period begins on the date People Operations receives the written resignation.

## 4. Company-initiated termination

For a company-initiated termination without cause, Acme will provide 30 calendar days of notice or pay in lieu of notice, subject to applicable law and the employee's written agreement.

## 5. Handover during notice

Employees serving notice must complete a documented handover, return company property, and remain available during normal working hours unless People Operations approves an earlier release.
