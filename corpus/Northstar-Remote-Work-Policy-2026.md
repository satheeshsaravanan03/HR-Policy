# NORTHSTAR REMOTE WORK POLICY 2026

Effective date: 2026-02-15

## 1. Scope and eligibility

Regular employees may request remote work after completing 60 days of service. Approval depends on role suitability, team coverage, security requirements, and manager review.

## 2. Remote-work schedule

An approved employee may work remotely up to three days per week. The employee must be available during the team core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 local time unless the manager approves an alternative schedule.

## 3. Equipment and security

Northstar supplies one company laptop and an approved security token. Employees must use the company VPN for internal systems and must not store confidential policy or customer data on a personal device.

## 4. Home-office expenses

Employees may claim up to 50 USD per month for internet service used for approved remote work. Claims require an itemized receipt and must be submitted by the tenth day of the following month.

## 5. Review and cancellation

Remote-work approvals are reviewed every six months. The company may suspend or cancel an arrangement when business needs, performance, security, or attendance requirements are not met.
