# Week 10 test questions and expected answers

Use these questions in **Week 10 Agent Race**. The expected wording below is a scoring guide, not text the model must repeat exactly. The facts and citations should match. Policy citations should include the real `chunk_id` returned by that run; chunk IDs are generated by the index and should not be copied from an old run.

## Four answerable questions

- **Question:** How many annual leave days does an Acme full-time employee receive per calendar year?
  - **Expected context:** `ACME-LEAVE-2026`, section 1 (eligibility) and section 2 (annual leave allowance), effective 2026-01-01.
  - **Expected answer:** Eligible regular full-time employees receive **18 days per calendar year** after completing 30 calendar days of service. Employees joining after July 1 receive a prorated allowance. Contractors and temporary workers follow their engagement letters.
  - **Citation requirement:** Cite `ACME-LEAVE-2026`, section 2 for the 18-day rule; cite section 1 if mentioning eligibility.
  - **Team path:** Policy Research → Evidence Review → Manager answer. Employee and transaction agents are not needed.

- **Question:** How many unused Acme annual-leave days may be carried into the next year?
  - **Expected context:** `ACME-LEAVE-2026`, section 3 (carry-forward), effective 2026-01-01.
  - **Expected answer:** Up to **10 unused annual-leave days** may be carried into the next calendar year. Days over the cap expire on March 31 unless People Operations approves a written exception.
  - **Citation requirement:** Cite `ACME-LEAVE-2026`, section 3, with the actual retrieved `chunk_id`.
  - **Team path:** Policy Research → Evidence Review → Manager answer.

- **Question:** For non-technical staff, how many unused leaves can be carried over to the next year?
  - **Expected context:** `SS-HB-2025`, section 9.3, “Leaves Encashment.” The handbook states the leave carry-over rule for non-technical staff and encashment for technical teams; do not substitute an Acme or Azure policy.
  - **Expected answer:** Non-technical staff may carry over a maximum of **nine unused leaves** into the next year. For technical teams, unused leaves are encashed at the day's salary. If the retrieved chunk does not support both details, state only the supported part.
  - **Citation requirement:** Cite `SS-HB-2025`, section 9.3, and the actual chunk ID returned by retrieval.
  - **Team path:** Policy Research → Evidence Review → Manager answer.

- **Question:** Show the policy-backed leave summary for EMP-002, including current balance, carry-forward and compensatory leave.
  - **Expected context (employee data):** `data/employee_records.json` lists EMP-002 as an Azure employee with 2.5 years of service and a current leave balance of 14 days. `data/leave_transactions.json` lists 3 compensatory-leave days; leave-filed/used/pending fields are null, so do not invent those values.
  - **Expected context (policy):** `AZURE-HR-2026`, sections 2–4. At 2.5 years, the confirmed-employee rules apply: 24 annual-leave days, carry-forward cap 10, and compensatory-leave cap 6. The available compensatory balance cannot exceed the recorded 3 days.
  - **Expected answer:** Current leave balance **14 days**; annual entitlement **24 days**; carry-forward **up to 10 days** based on the policy cap and recorded balance; compensatory leave **3 days**. Identify the balance/transaction values as coming from the employee record and the limits as coming from the policy.
  - **Citation requirement:** Cite real `AZURE-HR-2026` chunks/sections supporting the entitlement, carry-forward rule, and compensatory rule (sections 2, 3, and 4 as applicable). Do not claim citations exist if the run did not return them.
  - **Team path:** Employee Directory and Policy Research can run independently; Record Validation checks the employee record; Policy Calculation follows validation; Evidence Review runs before the manager answers.

## Greeting and refusal controls

- **Question:** Hello.
  - **Expected answer:** “Hello! I can help with questions about the available HR policies and employee leave records.”
  - **Expected path:** Greeting pre-check; no policy retrieval, employee tool, A2A specialist task, or evidence reviewer. This is conversational input, not a policy answer to refuse.

- **Question:** What is SoftSuave's sabbatical leave entitlement?
  - **Expected answer:** Refuse to give an entitlement because the indexed source does not establish one. Say the corpus does not contain that rule and ask the user to provide an authoritative policy if they need it.
  - **Expected path:** Policy Research → Evidence Review rejects unsupported evidence → no invented answer.

## How to verify the run

1. Start the app from the repository folder with `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m streamlit run app.py`.
2. Select **Week 10 Agent Race**, enter a question above, and compare both answers. For policy answers, check policy ID, section, and the live chunk ID. For EMP-002, check the employee and transaction data separately from policy-derived limits.
3. For **Hello**, verify both sides respond politely without calling the policy evidence reviewer. If it shows `evidence_review_rejected`, that is a routing regression, not the correct greeting behavior.
4. For the sabbatical question, a refusal is correct. A status of `error` means execution failed; a status of `refused` means the evidence gate intentionally declined to answer. Review the displayed stop reason to distinguish them.
5. Run **Week 10 multi-agent race** in the sidebar to score the frozen five-case set. The deterministic score checks expected facts, refusal behavior, and citations; manually inspect the answer text as well.

The policy file establishes the rules; employee JSON establishes current synthetic records. A policy answer must not be presented as an employee-specific balance, and a record value must not be presented as a policy entitlement.
